The Solution Layer

findr. doesn't just diagnose risk — it prescribes the fix.

Generic advice is useless in complex B2B deals. findr. anchors every recommendation in the actual conversation: for each risk signal, you get a specific action, a concrete next step (who does what, by when), and the exact quote that triggered it.

No platitudes. No guesswork.
Every recommendation cites what the buyer actually said — so your team knows why this action matters, and can act with confidence.

And because deals move fast, the full solution report exports as a clean 1–2 page PDF — ready to drop into your next deal review or forward to Legal, Finance, or Product.
